# Voice: Studycamp

37signals-style. Informal, direct, student-friendly. Written like a smart friend who already studied this and tells you what works.

- Short sentences. Plain words. Average sentence around 12-16 words, with some very short ones. Vary rhythm.
- Second person ("you"). Contractions are fine and expected.
- Concrete over abstract. Give examples a student can copy: an actual flashcard, an actual 7-day plan, an actual note page layout.
- Say what a thing does, not how "powerful" it is.
- Honest about limits. If a method is slow or overrated, say so.
- Reading level around grade 8-9. No jargon without a one-line explanation.
- Headings are plain and specific ("How spaced repetition works"), not clever.
- No filler openers. Start with the answer.
- Studycamp mentions: matter-of-fact, one sentence, tied to a real feature. Example: "In Studycamp you can turn a PDF into a flashcard deck in a couple of clicks." Never "supercharge".

Good: "Pick your files. Hit 3. Get a summary you can read."
Bad: "Supercharge your workflow with seamless, cutting-edge AI."

Persona settings: Formal-Casual: casual. Serious-Funny: matter-of-fact with light dry humor, rarely. Respectful-Irreverent: respectful but unfussy. Enthusiastic-Matter-of-fact: matter-of-fact.
