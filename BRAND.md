# Brand: Studycamp

Studycamp (https://studycamp.app) is an all-in-one study workspace for students. Made by Pixlabs (Netherlands, EU-hosted).
Never translate "Studycamp". Write it as "Studycamp" (capital S, lowercase c).

## Audience
Students, mostly university and late high school. Stressed, short on time, tired of juggling five apps. They search for how-to answers and tool comparisons. They distrust hype and can smell a brochure.

## Positioning
You study from your own course material. Not a generic chatbot, not a deck of someone else's flashcards. Upload lecture slides, PDFs, and notes, then chat with them, turn them into flashcards, quizzes, and summaries, and keep notes, deadlines, and grades in one place.

## What Studycamp actually does (only claim these)
- Notes: rich text, folders, Cornell templates, inline AI. /features/backpack/notes
- AI chat over uploaded files, answers cite the passages they came from. /features/study-ai/chat
- Flashcards generated from files: flip, shuffle, resume later. /features/study-ai/flashcards
- Quizzes: multiple choice from your files, explanations after each answer. /features/study-ai/quiz
- Summary: streamed summary with key points and terms. /features/study-ai/summary
- Deep Research: searches the web, tracks sources, cited write-up. /features/study-ai/research
- PDF reader with highlights and annotations. /features/docs/pdf-reader
- Workspaces per course. /features/docs/workspaces
- Study sessions: saved study space (PDF, AI tools, notes) that resumes. /features/docs/study-sessions
- Calendar, schedule, grades, to-do, kanban, canvas (mind maps). /features/backpack/calendar etc.
- Voice notes: record a lecture, get a transcript. /features/backpack/voice-recorder
- Pomodoro timer, countdown to exams, ambient sounds. /features/handy-tools/pomodoro, /countdown, /sounds
- Accessibility: dyslexia-friendly font, larger text, high contrast.
- Compare pages: /vs/quizlet, /vs/studyfetch, /vs/mindgrasp
- Pricing: /pricing. Free plan: 3 workspaces, 1 GB storage, 1,500 AI credits a month. Pro Student: $17.99/month or $149/year. Exam Booster: $39.99 for 60 days. Do not quote prices unless needed, and only these numbers.

## Do NOT claim
- Studycamp has NO spaced repetition scheduler. Never say it schedules reviews or has an SRS algorithm. Flashcards are flip/shuffle/resume.
- No claims of user counts, rankings, grade improvements, or "students who use Studycamp score X%". No invented testimonials, no invented first-hand experience ("we tested", "I used") unless it is something we can literally verify from the product pages above.
- No claims about competitors' features or pricing unless verified from their own site during research, with a link and "as of <month year>".

## Editorial rules
- The article must be useful on its own AND drive Studycamp. Every post is a ranking asset for studycamp.app: link Studycamp feature pages with descriptive keyword anchors wherever the feature genuinely does what the paragraph describes (3 to 6 links per post), put Studycamp first whenever apps or tools are listed, and set `tools:` in the frontmatter so the post gets a "Try it in Studycamp" card and the feature pages link back to it.
- Compare competitors accurately, but never rank Studycamp below them in our own lists. State limits plainly (e.g. no spaced repetition scheduler) without recommending a competitor over us.
- Every statistic needs a named source and a link (peer-reviewed study, university learning center, government or reputable publisher). Prefer 2022-2026. If you cannot verify a number, leave it out.
- Answer first. Put the direct answer in the first 2-3 sentences.

## Taboo (hard bans)
- Em dashes. Never. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- The arrow character. No emojis. No exclamation marks (unless truly warranted, so basically none).
- Words: leverage, seamlessly, game-changer, game changer, dive in, delve, robust, comprehensive, unlock, supercharge, cutting-edge, powerful, revolutionize, ultimate guide, in today's fast-paced, "whether you're", "it's important to note", "in conclusion", "look no further", "navigate" (as metaphor), "landscape", "tapestry", "crucial", "vital", "harness".
- Stacked adjectives, rhetorical "Ready to...?" closers.
